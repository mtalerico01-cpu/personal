# Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced

## Definitions for Product Logic

- Beginner: new, returning after long break, or inconsistent training history. Prioritize simple full-body or upper/lower structures.
- Intermediate: consistent training for roughly 6+ months. Can tolerate more split specificity and volume.
- Advanced: years of consistent training. Requires more individualized volume, progression, and recovery handling.

## Source IDs

- `SRC-ACSM-RT-2009`

## Implementation Rule

Training experience should change confidence and default volume/frequency; it should not be used to gate all custom choices.
