# Training Frequency

## Implementation Rules

- Use experience, goal, schedule, recovery, and constraints to set training frequency.
- ACSM guidance supports defaults of:
  - novice: 2-3 days/week
  - intermediate: 3-4 days/week
  - advanced: 4-5 days/week
- The app should not force the maximum frequency when availability or recovery says otherwise.
- Frequency recommendation should include confidence and rationale.
