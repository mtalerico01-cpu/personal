# Training Volume

## Implementation Rules

- Initial training volume should scale by experience, recovery, session length, and goal.
- Beginner plans should prioritize consistency, technique, and tolerable volume.
- Intermediate and advanced plans can use more sets, exercise variety, and periodized loading.
- Volume should be adjusted after adherence, soreness, performance, and recovery feedback.
