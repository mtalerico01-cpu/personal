# Cardio Guidelines

## Implementation Rules

- Adult health baseline should consider 150 minutes/week moderate-intensity aerobic activity, 75 minutes/week vigorous-intensity activity, or an equivalent combination, plus 2 days/week muscle strengthening.
- For low-activity users, initial cardio should be conservative and adherence-focused.
- For endurance/cardio goals, cardio should be a primary plan domain, not an accessory after fat loss logic.
- Cardio prescriptions should include mode, duration, intensity, weekly schedule, progression, and constraints.
