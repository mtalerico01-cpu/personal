# Weight Change Rates

## Implementation Rules

- For fat loss, default to gradual, steady rates unless the user and safety profile support otherwise (`WEIGHT-001`).
- CDC states people who lose weight at about 1-2 lb/week are more likely to keep weight off than people who lose faster (`SRC-CDC-WEIGHT-LOSS-2025`).
- Faster user preference should reduce confidence and may trigger caution.
- The plan should review progress using multi-day or weekly trends rather than single-day weight changes.

## Product Assumption

For Form Theory, initial recommendations should be conservative and reviewed after 2 weeks unless safety or user feedback requires earlier adjustment.
