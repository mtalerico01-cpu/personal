# Carbohydrate Guidelines

## Implementation Rules

- Adult carbohydrate RDA context is 130 g/day (`SRC-NASEM-DRI-RDA-2005`).
- Adult AMDR context is 45-65% of energy (`SRC-NASEM-DRI-MACROS-2005`).
- Carbohydrate should be personalized after protein and fat constraints are set.
- Endurance, sport, high training volume, and user preference can justify higher carbohydrate targets.
- Lower-carbohydrate preference should be treated as a macro preference and should include a warning when it may conflict with performance goals.
