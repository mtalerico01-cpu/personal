# Safety Boundaries

## Required Safety Inputs

- Age under 18
- Pregnancy
- Breastfeeding / lactation
- Eating disorder history or active disordered eating concern
- Medically prescribed diet
- Clinician-directed calorie, protein, sodium, fluid, renal, diabetes, or cardiac restriction
- Severe injury or pain-limited movement
- Chest pain, fainting, unexplained shortness of breath, or other urgent exercise red flags

## App Behavior

- High-risk inputs should prevent aggressive calorie, macro, or training prescriptions.
- Some inputs should block activation entirely and recommend clinician guidance.
- Caution-level inputs should produce conservative defaults, warnings, and lower confidence.
- Safety constraints must carry through onboarding, profile, plan, coach, and profile edits.
