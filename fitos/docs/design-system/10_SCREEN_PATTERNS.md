# 10 - Screen Patterns

Date created: 2026-07-02

## Authentication

Primary question: Can I enter or restore my account quickly and confidently?

Hierarchy:
1. Brand mark/name.
2. Sign-in or account action.
3. Form fields.
4. Error/recovery.
5. Legal/supporting copy.

## Onboarding

Primary question: What does Form Theory need from me right now, and why?

Hierarchy:
1. Current question.
2. Plain-language reason or safety note.
3. Choices/input.
4. Continue/skip.
5. Progress context.

## Dashboard

Primary question: What matters most today?

Hierarchy:
1. Daily priority.
2. Coach insight or next action.
3. Hero metric when relevant.
4. Supporting KPI grid.
5. Training/nutrition/progress detail.

## Nutrition

Primary question: What should I do next to meet today's nutrition target?

Hierarchy:
1. Remaining actionable target.
2. Meal logging action.
3. Calories/protein/macros.
4. Meals.
5. Supplements/details.

## Training

Primary question: What is today's training action?

Hierarchy:
1. Today's workout or generate action.
2. Current session controls.
3. Exercise sequence.
4. Modifications.
5. History/detail.

## Cardio

Primary question: What cardio action supports my plan today?

Hierarchy:
1. Planned session or steps target.
2. Duration/intensity/activity.
3. Recent trend.
4. Recovery context.
5. History/detail.

## Progress

Primary question: Am I moving in the intended direction?

Hierarchy:
1. Current value.
2. Trend and comparison period.
3. Interpretation and confidence.
4. Chart.
5. Detailed history.

## Coach

Primary question: What can Form Theory help me decide or do now?

Hierarchy:
1. Contextual prompt/brief.
2. Conversation or response.
3. Proposed action.
4. Confirmation/recovery.
5. Suggested prompts.

## Profile/Settings

Primary question: What can I review or change safely?

Hierarchy:
1. Identity/account context.
2. Goals/profile facts.
3. Training/nutrition preferences.
4. Coach/appearance/privacy.
5. Integrations/account actions.
