# 15 - Microcopy Guide

Date created: 2026-07-02

## Voice

Calm, confident, direct, supportive, practical, specific, nonjudgmental, concise.

## Use

- Plain language before fitness jargon.
- Specific next actions.
- Evidence-aware uncertainty.
- Respectful safety language.
- Context: period, target, comparison, consequence.

## Avoid

- Hype.
- Guilt or shame.
- Bro-science.
- Influencer phrasing.
- Robotic AI terminology.
- Exaggerated certainty.
- Empty motivation.
- Unexplained abbreviations.

## Examples

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Protein is the main target remaining today. | Destroy your protein goal. |
| Your current pace is slightly above the planned range. | You are failing your target. |
| This trend needs more history before Form Theory can call it meaningful. | Your progress is definitely stalling. |
| Try again, or continue editing without leaving this screen. | Something went wrong. |
| Your plan is using your manual targets. | AI optimized your macros. |
| Recovery looks lower today, so keep intensity controlled. | Your body is not ready. |

## Major States

### Empty

Pattern: missing data + why it matters + action.

Example: `No workout is planned for today. Build a session from your goal, schedule, and equipment.`

### Loading

Pattern: what is happening + preserve context when possible.

Example: `Building your starting plan from your goal, training schedule, and nutrition targets.`

### Error

Pattern: what failed + recovery + reassurance when data is preserved.

Example: `Your workout could not be saved. Try again, or continue editing without leaving this screen.`

### Safety

Pattern: calm boundary + reason + next step.

Example: `Form Theory will not create a calorie deficit from this information. Use maintenance targets until a clinician-guided plan is available.`
