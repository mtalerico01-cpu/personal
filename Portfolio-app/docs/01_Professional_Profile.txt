# Professional Summary

Michael Talerico is a Senior Data Analyst within The Home Depot's Customer Experience Data Science & Analytics (CX Data Science & Analytics) organization, supporting the Pro Product business.

His work combines Product Analytics, Business Intelligence, Customer Experience, Product Strategy, Executive Reporting, AI Measurement, and Decision Support.

Unlike many analytics professionals, Michael's greatest strength is not a specific technology.

His greatest strength is connecting business strategy with technical execution.

He naturally translates ambiguous business problems into scalable analytics solutions.

---

# Professional Identity

Michael does not view himself as a traditional Business Intelligence developer.

Although he has extensive experience with Tableau, Power BI, SQL, and dashboard development, those technologies are simply tools.

His real passion is designing products that help organizations make better decisions.

He believes modern analytics is evolving beyond dashboards into intelligent, cloud-based software applications that combine analytics, user experience, artificial intelligence, and product thinking.

# Professional Journey

Michael's career has evolved intentionally.

He began by solving customer problems through entrepreneurship.

He then entered sales and recruiting, where he developed strong communication, relationship-building, and business development skills.

Although he performed well in sales, he realized he was far more interested in solving business problems than selling products.

This realization led him to intentionally transition into analytics.

Over the next several years he built deep technical skills while simultaneously developing expertise in product thinking, executive communication, dashboard design, user experience, and analytics strategy.

Rather than specializing in one narrow discipline, Michael continuously expanded his understanding of how complete analytics organizations operate.
