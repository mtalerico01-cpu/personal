# Case Studies

## Purpose

This document serves as the master index for all professional case studies featured throughout Michael Talerico's portfolio.

The purpose of these case studies is not to showcase technical skills alone, but to demonstrate how Michael approaches business problems, product strategy, analytics, software development, leadership, and cross-functional collaboration.

Each case study should emphasize:

- Business context
- Problem definition
- Michael's role
- Solution design
- Collaboration
- Business impact
- Lessons learned

Whenever possible, projects should focus on decision-making rather than technologies.

Technologies support the story—they should never become the story.

# Confidentiality

All public-facing case studies should respect employer confidentiality.

Do not publish:

- Proprietary dashboards
- Internal SQL
- Sensitive customer data
- Confidential financial metrics
- Internal roadmaps
- Non-public product details

Whenever necessary, recreate visuals using mock data, generalized metrics, or conceptual diagrams while preserving the overall business story.
