Business Context
While attending Troy University, Michael co-founded QuickFix Delivery LLC in Troy, Alabama.

The business was created after recognizing a practical problem in the local market: students and members of the surrounding rural community wanted convenient access to fast food and restaurant delivery, but delivery infrastructure was limited or unavailable.

Michael and his co-founder identified the opportunity as college students with limited money but a clear understanding of the customer pain point. Students were busy with classes, social activities, late-night events, and campus life, and many did not want to drive to pick up food.

QuickFix Delivery was created to solve that problem.

Business Overview
QuickFix Delivery provided local restaurant and fast food delivery services in Troy, Alabama.

The company served college students, local residents, and nearby businesses by coordinating food orders and deliveries through a locally operated delivery model.

The business initially operated primarily with cash payments before later adopting Square card readers to accept card payments.

Michael hired local college students, including fraternity members, to serve as delivery drivers and help fulfill orders.
