# Career Timeline

This document provides a chronological overview of Michael Talerico's professional career.

Its purpose is to help AI assistants understand how Michael's career evolved over time so future content accurately reflects his experience and professional progression.

# 2016–2018 | Co-Founder | QuickFix Delivery LLC

While attending Troy University, Michael co-founded QuickFix Delivery LLC in Troy, Alabama.

The business was created after recognizing that students and members of the local community had limited access to food delivery services.

Over approximately two years Michael helped:

- Launch the business
- Develop operational processes
- Hire and manage college student delivery drivers
- Coordinate daily operations
- Build relationships with local restaurants
- Improve customer experience
- Scale operations while attending school full time

Initially the business accepted cash payments before later implementing Square card readers.

The company was successfully sold after graduation in 2018.

Although relatively small, this experience established Michael's passion for entrepreneurship, customer experience, operations, and solving real business problems.

# 2018 | Insight Global | Recruiting & Sales
